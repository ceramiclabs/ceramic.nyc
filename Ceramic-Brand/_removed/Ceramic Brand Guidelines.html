Who we are

Ceramic is a small, senior team that embeds into one organization and becomes its product-and-engineering function for the engagement. Greenfield AI and end-to-end software we own the outcome on — not a platform we're paid to install.

Independent, tech-agnostic, and deliberately scarce: two or three engagements a year. The scarcity is the product. We borrow the language of the forward-deployed-engineer wave for context, then refuse the label — we're the opposite of staff augmentation.

Forward-deployed

  • An engineer shipped in from a big vendor
  • Lands a predetermined platform
  • Scales on volume — hundreds of seats
  • You're one account of many

Ceramic

  • The whole founding team, embedded
  • Tech-agnostic — solves the human problem
  • Scarce & bespoke — 2–3 a year
  • Total ownership of the outcome

How we say it

Not an engineer we send in. The founding team you didn't hire.
The frontier labs will send you forward-deployed engineers. We become your company for a quarter.
Bespoke AI, built into the core of how you work — by the team that owns the outcome, not the vendor that owns the platform.
Let us be your force multiplier.

Logo

The wordmark is Ceramic set in Encode Sans Condensed SemiBold with tight optical tracking. White on the dark gradient is primary; black on light neutral is the alternate.

PRIMARY · white on dark
ALTERNATE · black on light

Do

  • Keep clear space of at least the cap-height on every side.
  • Lead with white on the dark gradient canvas.
  • Anchor it quietly — top-left, small, confident.
  • Scale proportionally; 80px+ wide in headers.

Don't

  • Recolor it in the accent or a project color.
  • Stretch, re-track, or re-weight the letterforms.
  • Add shadows, outlines, or gradients to the mark.
  • Set it on busy imagery without a dark scrim.

Color

A near-black canvas, white type, and a single cool accent — light blue — carrying all the emphasis. Everything else is white at varying opacity.

Voice & tone

Confident, terse, and a little wry. Craft language over corporate. The name is a material — expect heat, kiln, and glaze. We say what we do plainly, then undercut the swagger with a wink.

01

Product-forward

We build with intent. Every decision measured against real user needs and business impact.

02

AI-native

Old-school craft, new-school tools. Engineering rigor fused with AI-powered execution.

03

Human-centered

Technology serves people. We augment human capability — we don't replace it.

The rule

Short declaratives. A craft or material metaphor where it lands. One dry joke per page — see the footer: "No BPA, PFAS, lots of heavy metal." Never buzzword stacks, never hype for its own sake, never "forward-deployed."
